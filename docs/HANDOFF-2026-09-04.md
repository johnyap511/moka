## Rules the data follows (decided by management)
- eZee is the source of truth. Match a reservation on hotel + RES + folio + unit; a stay is `BookingSplitter::stayChain`.
- eZee's amounts are final: the hourly reconcile (`ezee:auto-assign`) reprices any linked booking whose room charge, cleaning fee or cleaning SST differs, and logs it ("Repriced from EZEE"). Dates never follow silently: review item.
- Cleaning fee = eZee's cleaning/channel charge lines only (stored breakdown `ezee_bookings.extra_charges`); never deposits or incidentals; SST(CF) = the tax eZee applied (Agoda "channel" surcharge has none).
- Revenue is by calendar month: one booking row per month (splitByMonth); cleaning on the first segment; channel fee follows the nights; SST 8% on top; Long Term Rental carries SST.
- Channels: PMS, Walk-in, Google, Internet, Booking Engine all report as "Website" at the 8% fee. Monthly Rental and LTR: no fee.
- Extra rooms are company listings (owner user 4475), may hold two guests a night. Never cancel in bulk from a pull; cancellations come from the notification feed (`ezee:notifications`) or eZee's reports.
