## Rules the data follows (decided by management)
- eZee is the source of truth. Match a reservation on hotel + RES + folio + unit; a stay is `BookingSplitter::stayChain`.
- eZee's amounts are final: the hourly reconcile (`ezee:auto-assign`) reprices any linked booking whose room charge, cleaning fee or cleaning SST differs, and logs it ("Repriced from EZEE"). Dates never follow silently: review item.
- Cleaning fee = eZee's "Cleaning Fee" line (stored breakdown `ezee_bookings.extra_charges`); a "channel" surcharge line stands in only when the folio has no cleaning line (Agoda). Never add both; never deposits or incidentals. SST(CF) = the tax eZee applied on that line.
- Revenue is by calendar month: one booking row per month (splitByMonth); cleaning on the first segment; channel fee follows the nights; SST 8% on top; Long Term Rental carries SST.
- Channels: PMS, Walk-in, Google, Internet, Booking Engine all report as "Website" at the 8% fee. Monthly Rental and LTR: no fee.
- Extra rooms are company listings (owner user 4475), may hold two guests a night. Never cancel in bulk from a pull; cancellations come from the notification feed (`ezee:notifications`) or eZee's reports.

## August data audit — final (4 Sep 2026, evening)

Checked all 1,040 eZee-linked August bookings against the five eZee revenue reports and the extra-charge files.
Result: 1,038 exact; the 2 left are split-stay pieces (RES6075, RES6317) where the cleaning fee sits on the second

Rules learned (all applied, logged in storage/logs/price-repair-2026-09-04.csv):
- eZee folio numbers repeat across hotels (FN4140 exists at Alinea and Arte) — always key by hotel + folio.
- Alinea folio numbers are not the TransactionId suffix; Forum/Bell/Eko/Arte are. Six July stays carried a
  server_folio_no that belonged to a later booking; cleared (Forum/Bell) or corrected from the report (RES3947 → FN4101).
- Cleaning fee = eZee "Cleaning Fee*" line, else the "Channel" line; damage deposits (RES31053) and late check-out
  charges (RES6436) are never cleaning. Where the eZee report bills room only, MOKA cleaning is 0.
- SST(CF) only where eZee taxed the cleaning line (Agoda "channel" lines carry none).
